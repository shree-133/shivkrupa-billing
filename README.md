# Shivkrupa Jwellers — Billing App

Free hosting for the Shivkrupa Jwellers billing app: jewellery billing, customer accounts, old-metal exchange, split payments, business analysis and backups.

## Finish setup (one step)

The app file is large (~470 KB), so it must be uploaded by hand:

1. Download `shivkrupa-billing-pwa.zip` from the chat and unzip it.
2. In this repo, click **Add file → Upload files**.
3. Drag in **all five files**: `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`.
4. Click **Commit changes**.

Your app then goes live at:

**https://shree-133.github.io/shivkrupa-billing/**

Open that link on your phone → **Data** tab → **Install app** to add it to your home screen. It then opens full screen and works offline.

## Important

Your bills, customers and settings are stored inside the browser for that address. Always open the app from the same link, and download a backup regularly from the **Data** tab.
